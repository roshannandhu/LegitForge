Your customers already message you on WhatsApp. The question is who answers at 11 at night, and who types the same reply for the fortieth time. A WhatsApp bot can take most of that work. Not all of it.

## What a bot does well

The best jobs for a bot are the questions you answer the same way every time:

- **"Are you open?"** Hours, location, a map pin.
- **"How much is…?"** Price lists, packages, what is included.
- **"Is my order ready?"** Order status, read from your own records.
- **"Can I book?"** Available slots, confirmed on the spot, with a reminder the day before.

It replies in seconds, day and night, in the same polite way every time. Customers get an answer while they are still interested.

## What it should hand to a person

A good bot knows when to stop. It should pass the chat to you, with the conversation so far, when someone:

- complains, or sounds upset
- asks something unusual (a custom order, a discount, a large job)
- asks for a person

You see the message on your own phone, and the customer never has to repeat themselves.

## What it needs from you

To answer like you, the bot needs your answers once: prices, hours, the questions people really ask. We write them together in one session. If the bot should read live data, such as orders, stock or bookings, we connect it to where that data already lives: a sheet, your billing software or a small database.

## The rules you cannot skip

WhatsApp is strict, and that protects your number:

- You use the official **WhatsApp Business API**, never a hacked app. Unofficial tools get numbers banned.
- The bot only **replies** to people who messaged you, or who agreed to hear from you.
- Every message offers a way out: reply **STOP** and it stops.

## Where to start

Pick the one question that costs you the most time and automate that first. Most of our clients start with enquiries and order status, then add bookings or quotes a month later.
